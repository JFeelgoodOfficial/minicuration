Shipping, Returns & Contact

Everything you need to know about getting your print and what happens if something goes wrong.

Shipping

We ship to addresses in the United States only. We don’t ship internationally.

Orders are processed and ship within 5–7 business days of purchase, and we’ll email you when your order is on its way.

Shipping is charged once per order. Orders of only unlimited cards ship by letter mail for $2, untracked. Orders with a limited edition, a case or a stand ship as a tracked package for $7. Orders of $50 or more ship free.

Pre-orders

Cards marked pre-order are priced 25% off and ship on or before November 1, 2026. You are charged at checkout, and we email you the day your pre-order goes out. If we cannot meet that date we will tell you before it arrives, and you may cancel any pre-order for a full refund at any time before it ships. If your order also holds in-stock cards, those ship within 5–7 business days in their own package; shipping is charged once.

Returns & Exchanges

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 14 days of delivery and we will work with you to make it right. Items must be returned in their original, unopened condition to be eligible for a refund.

To initiate a return or exchange, email support@minicuration.com with your order number and the reason for your return. We will respond within 2 business days with return instructions.

Print Quality Guarantee

Every Minicuration print leaves our hands in perfect condition. If your print arrives damaged — cracked case, bent card, or visible print defect — contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the damage and we will give you store credit.

Email support@minicuration.com with your order number and a clear photo of the damage.

Privacy

Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. We use your name, email and shipping address only to fulfil and ship your order and to answer your questions.

We use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to see how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and which cards are added to carts. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. You can opt out with Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. If you sign up for our newsletter, we use your email address only to send it.

Contact

For any questions about your order, the prints, or anything else:

Email: support@minicuration.com

We respond to all inquiries within 2 business days, usually sooner.
