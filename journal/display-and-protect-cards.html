Care & Display

How to display and protect your cards

4 min read  ·  Published September 2026

A Minicuration card is 2.5 × 3.5 inches, exactly the size of a standard trading card. That is good news: the whole world of trading card storage and display fits it, and a few habits will keep it looking the way it did the day it arrived.

What your card arrives in

Limited editions ship sealed in a magnetic acrylic collector’s case with an acrylic display stand. The case protects the card from dust and fingerprints and stands upright on its stand, so it is ready to display out of the box.

Unlimited cards ship in a protective plastic slip. It keeps the surface clean in the mail and in a drawer. To display one, add a magnetically sealed acrylic case ($4) or an acrylic display stand ($1) when you check out, one of each per card.

Displaying your cards

One card, one stand. A single cased card on a desk or bookshelf works like a very small framed painting. Put it at eye level, where it will actually be looked at.

A row tells a story. Several cased cards side by side on a shelf ledge read as a small exhibition. The Self Work series, for example, was painted as a set.

Rotate. Keep most of a collection in a binder and a few favorites out on stands, then swap them with the seasons. The cards you put away stay protected, and the ones on display feel new again.

Keeping a card looking new

Care checklist

Keep it out of direct sunlight. Long exposure to sun fades printed color, even behind acrylic.
Avoid heat and damp. Skip bathrooms, radiators and car dashboards; humidity can warp card stock.
Handle it by the edges, with clean, dry hands, if you take it out of its slip or case.
Store it flat or upright, never bent. A slip plus a toploader or binder page keeps it straight.
Clean the case, not the card. Wipe acrylic with a soft, dry cloth; don’t use cleaning sprays on the card itself.

Common Questions

FAQ

Will a Minicuration card fit standard trading card sleeves?

Yes. The cards are 2.5 × 3.5 inches, the standard trading card size, so standard sleeves, toploaders and 9-pocket binder pages fit them.

What comes with a limited edition?

A magnetic acrylic collector’s case and an acrylic display stand.

What comes with an unlimited card?

A protective plastic slip. You can add a magnetically sealed acrylic case for $4 and an acrylic display stand for $1 at checkout, one of each per card.

Can I take the card out of the magnetic case?

Yes. The case opens on a magnetic closure. Handle the card by its edges and keep it flat.

Where should I not display a card?

In direct sunlight, or anywhere hot or damp: sun fades printed color over time and humidity can warp card stock.
