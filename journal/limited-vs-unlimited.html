Limited edition or unlimited? How the two collections differ

4 min read  ·  Published September 2026

Every Minicuration card is a reproduction of an original JFeelgood painting at 2.5 × 3.5 inches, the size of a trading card. The cards come in two collections, and the difference between them is about scarcity, what’s on the back, and what the card arrives in.

Limited editions: fifty, then never again

Each limited design is printed as exactly 50 numbered cards. The number is printed on the back, next to the artist’s statement about the painting: the medium, the idea and the story behind it, in JFeelgood’s own words. When all 50 of a design are gone, that design is closed for good.

Limited editions ship sealed in a magnetic acrylic collector’s case with a display stand, ready to stand on a shelf the day it arrives. Because each number belongs to one physical card, your exact edition number is confirmed by email when your card is packed.

The limited collection includes the original six designs and the Self Work series, among others.

Unlimited cards: the archive, open

The unlimited collection is an open edition of paintings from nearly two decades of JFeelgood’s archive. The cards are not numbered and they stay available, so there’s no rush and no sold-out page.

The back shows the painting’s title with the artist’s name and minicuration.com. Unlimited cards ship in a protective plastic slip. If you want one standing on a desk, add a magnetically sealed acrylic case or an acrylic display stand at checkout, one of each per card.

They’re also built for collecting in quantity: buy 10 unlimited pieces for $50, mixed however you like, and the order ships free.

Which one is right for you?

Choose a limited edition if…

You want a numbered piece of a specific painting, a card that will never be reprinted, or a gift that arrives ready to display, case and stand included.

Choose unlimited cards if…

You want to fill a binder page or a shelf, try the format for the first time, give several small gifts at once, or collect a wide spread of the archive without the pressure of an edition selling out.

Or mix them

Both collections go in the same cart. Many collectors anchor a shelf with a limited edition in its case and build around it with unlimited cards.

Common Questions

FAQ

Are both collections reproductions of original paintings?

Yes. Every card, limited or unlimited, reproduces an original JFeelgood painting at trading card size.

Can I buy more than one of the same limited edition?

Each order can hold one card of each limited design. Unlimited cards have no limit.

Do unlimited cards come with a case?

They ship in a protective plastic slip. A magnetically sealed acrylic case and an acrylic display stand are available as add-ons at checkout, one of each per card.

How do I know my edition number?

Edition numbers belong to physical cards, so the number is confirmed by email when your limited edition is packed. It is also printed on the back of the card.

What does the unlimited bundle include?

Any 10 unlimited cards for $50, mixed however you like, and the order ships free. Every further 10 in the same order saves another $10.
